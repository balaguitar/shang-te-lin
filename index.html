01 / About

Music made with
story at its core.

Shang-Te Lin is a Taiwan-based artist, composer, producer, songwriter, and music educator affiliated with Harvest Music 大禾音樂. Moving between film, recorded music, and the classroom, his work is grounded in emotional clarity, close collaboration, and a lifelong commitment to the craft of music.
